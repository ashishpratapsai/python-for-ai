INSTITURA COACHING INSTITUTE — STUDENT HANDBOOK 2026

CHAPTER 1: ADMISSION PROCESS
Students must complete the online application form available on our website. The admission process requires submission of previous academic records, a valid ID proof, and payment of the registration fee. After document verification, students will receive a confirmation email within 3 working days. The institute offers two main batches: IIT-JEE preparation and NEET preparation.

CHAPTER 2: ACADEMIC SCHEDULE
Classes are held Monday through Saturday from 7 AM to 9 AM and 5 PM to 8 PM. Sunday sessions are reserved for doubt clearing and mock tests. Each subject has dedicated faculty members who specialize in IIT-JEE or NEET examination patterns. Students are expected to maintain 75 percent attendance to be eligible for examinations.

CHAPTER 3: ASSESSMENT AND GRADING
Monthly tests are conducted on the last Saturday of each month. The grading system is as follows: scores above 90 earn an A grade, scores between 75 and 90 earn a B grade, scores between 40 and 75 earn a C grade, and scores below 40 result in an F grade. Students scoring below 40 are placed on academic probation and must attend additional remedial sessions.

CHAPTER 4: FEE STRUCTURE
The annual fee for IIT-JEE batch is 85000 rupees and for NEET batch is 90000 rupees. Fees can be paid in two installments. The first installment of 50 percent is due at the time of admission and the second installment is due by January 15. Students with pending fees after the due date will be charged a late fee of 500 rupees per month. Fee concessions of up to 20 percent are available for students scoring above 95 in their previous examinations.

CHAPTER 5: STUDENT SUPPORT
The institute provides one-on-one mentoring sessions every Friday. Students can book appointments through the student portal. Each student is assigned a personal mentor who tracks their progress and provides guidance. Parents receive monthly progress reports via WhatsApp and email. The institute also provides study materials, practice tests, and access to an online question bank with over 10000 questions.
